Winter arrives faster than most of us would like. That is why we are already
preparing our **Winter Relief 2026**.

The plan: 400 high-quality sleeping bags, insulated mats and warm meals at three
distribution points in the city centre. Every donation goes straight into this
project — you can always see the current total on the [donation page](/en/spenden).

Want to help in person? We are still looking for volunteers for the evening
shifts. Get in touch via the [contact form](/en/kontakt).
